This directory contains scripts to:
1. Extract orthologous upstream regions for motif scanning (00_ExtractUpstreamOrthogroupSequence.sh), given orthogroups generated by Hsu et al 2025 (in prep)
2. Scan upstream regions for motifs, then collapse into JASPAR-defined clusters (01_ScanUpstream-JASPAR2024.sh)
3. Collapse motifs by JASPAR-delineated cluster and summarize by orthogroup (02_PrepMotifsForModeling)

Recommended citations for key tools/resources:
- JASPAR database: JASPAR database: Rauluseviciute I, Riudavets-Puig R, Blanc-Mathieu R, Castro-Mondragon JA, Ferenc K, Kumar V, Lemma RB, Lucas J, Chèneby J, Baranasic D, et al. 2023. JASPAR 2024: 20th anniversary of the open-access database of transcription factor binding profiles. Nucleic Acids Res. [Internet]. Available from: http://dx.doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad1059
- FIMO: Grant CE, Bailey TL, Noble WS. 2011. FIMO: scanning for occurrences of a given motif. Bioinformatics 27:1017–1018.
- bedtools: Quinlan AR, Hall IM. 2010. BEDTools: a flexible suite of utilities for comparing genomic features. Bioinformatics 26:841–842.
