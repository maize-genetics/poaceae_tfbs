00_ScanUMRs.sh: Scans true and shuffled UMRs and ACRs for JASPAR motifs
01_CalculateEnrichment.R : Calculates enrichment scores for each motif
02_CompareEnrichmentAcrossSpecies: Quantifies UMR/ACR enrichment similarity across species and builds figures
03_ScanOtherFeatures.sh: Scans empirical and background maize regulatory features (UMRs, scATAC, CNS, MOA) for motifs.
04_PlotMaizeFeatureEnrichment.Rmd: Plots enrichment comparison across maize regulatory features using different background strategies.
